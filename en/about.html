About us

Zhongshi Wenxuan Technology (Fuzhou) Co., Ltd. is a company registered in mainland China, focused on Technology development for mobile applications and internet content.

Company overview

Zhongshi Wenxuan Technology (Fuzhou) Co., Ltd. was established in July 2025 and is registered at Room 1220, Building 12, No.76 Xiaohoushan, Houshan Village, Nanyu Town, Minhou County, Fuzhou, Fujian, China. The company works across software development, data processing and internet information services.

Our team covers product, engineering, data and operations, and can take a project from requirements and system design through to launch and operations. We run projects with engineering discipline and iterate on data and user feedback.

The company holds internet information service and second-class value-added telecommunications licences, and provides internet content services and platform operations within that approved scope; all other activities fall within the general items of its business licence.

We take contract performance, intellectual property and user data protection seriously, and maintain long-term working relationships with our partners.
