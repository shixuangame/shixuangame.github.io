Privacy Policy

Last updated：2026-10-29

Zhongshi Wenxuan Technology (Fuzhou) Co., Ltd. ("we", "us") takes the protection of personal information and privacy seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect information when you use this website and related services.

1. Scope

This policy applies to this website (shixuangame.github.io) and to enquiries and partnership discussions we handle through it. It does not apply to third-party websites or services, which maintain their own privacy policies.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you provide: when you submit an enquiry, we receive the name, email address, enquiry type and message you enter.
  • Technical information collected automatically: browser type, device type, operating system, access time, pages visited and referring address, recorded in server logs.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: used to remember preferences and measure site traffic.

3. How we use information

  • To reply to your enquiry and handle partnership requests;
  • To improve site content and user experience;
  • To measure traffic and detect abusive or anomalous access;
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Cookies

This site may use cookies and similar technologies to remember session state and measure traffic. You can refuse or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some functionality may be affected.

5. Third-party services

To measure traffic and advertising performance, this site may embed analytics or advertising services provided by third parties (for example measurement and conversion tracking code from Meta or Google). Those third parties may collect and process information under their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

6. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We will not share it with third parties except:

  • with your explicit consent;
  • with service providers where necessary to deliver a service (under confidentiality obligations);
  • where required by law, or by a binding order from a judicial or administrative authority;
  • where necessary to protect the rights and interests of us, our users or the public.

7. Storage and security

Information we collect is stored in the People's Republic of China. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures, including access control, encryption in transit and least-privilege administration. We retain information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described above, unless a longer period is required by law.

8. Your rights

Using the contact details at the end of this policy, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or withdraw consent. We will act within a reasonable period after verifying your identity.

9. Minors

This site is intended for businesses and adult users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you are a guardian and believe we hold such information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as our business or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
